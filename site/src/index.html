Can We Go is home to exhibitions, gigs, restaurants, bars or just anywhere you mean to get to. No more shared Notes or WhatsApp links that get lost in the ether. Share an event from Safari, Instagram, TikTok or Maps, take a photo of a poster IRL or use the app’s native search function to find the latest from the Tate.

Keep a list with your partner or a group of friends and get reminders of the things you can do: events ending soon rise to the top and the places you want to go live on a map. Forgot what you did last weekend? A record of your exploits lives in the archive.

I’m Can, and I built Can We Go for my partner and I to keep track of exhibitions and events in the city that we love. I hope it gets you to a few more of the places you said you’d go to, too.
