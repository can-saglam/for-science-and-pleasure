# Support

Can We Go? is a shared list of places and events you mean to go to. Share a link or a screenshot into the app and it becomes a card with the dates, venue and a photo, in a library you keep together with the people you go out with.

## Help

- **Adding a save.** Use the Share button in Safari, Instagram, Maps or Photos and pick Can We Go?, or tap + in the app and paste a link.
- **Inviting someone.** Settings → My group → Invite. They enter the code when they first open the app.
- **Plus.** Settings → Can We Go? Plus. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
- **Restoring a purchase.** Settings → Can We Go? Plus → Restore Purchases.
- **Deleting your account.** Settings → Delete account. Your library is exported first.
