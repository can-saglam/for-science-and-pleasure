# Privacy Policy (draft)

This is a draft for Can We Go? Publish it (GitHub Pages or your site) and
put the live URL in the app. Until then, Settings shows this text in-app.

Can We Go? is a shared list of places and events you mean to go to.

## What we store

- Your Apple (or email) sign-in, so we know which library is yours.
- The cards you save: titles, dates, notes, links, photos you attach, and
  who in the group added them.
- A home city, if you set one, so dates and distances read in that city.
- A device token, if you allow notifications, so the other person can be
  told when you save something.

We do not sell this. We do not run ads. We do not track you across other
apps.

## Who can see a save

People in your group. If you leave a shared group, their copies stay;
yours come with you only when the leave flow says so. If you are the last
member and you delete the account, that group’s library is removed.

## Photos and links

A screenshot or link you share or paste is sent to our parse function so
we can turn it into a card for you to confirm. That happens as soon as
you share into the app while signed in; signed out, nothing is sent.
Nothing is kept until you tap Save.

## Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account exports your library first, then removes your
login. A shared library is left for the people still in it. A solo library
is deleted with you.

## Contact

The developer who signed the app. Reply to the TestFlight or App Store
listing until a public address is published here.
