# Terms of Use

Can We Go? is a shared list for you and the people you go out with. You are responsible for what you save and for who you invite.

- Invite only people you want in that library.
- Do not use the app to store anyone else’s private data without their say-so.
- We may refuse or rate-limit parse and invite traffic that looks like abuse.
- The service is provided as-is. A failed parse is not a lost evening — you can still save the link or photo as-is.

## Free and Plus

Can We Go? is free for you on your own or with one other person, with up to four upcoming saves in each category. Can We Go? Plus removes that limit and lets up to four people share a library. One member’s subscription covers the whole group.

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. It renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. If an offer includes a free trial, any unused part of it ends when you buy a subscription.

Purchases are also covered by Apple’s standard [Licensed Application End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

## Your account

Deleting your account is in Settings. What happens to a shared library is spelled out there before you confirm.
