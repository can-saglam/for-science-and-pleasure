# Terms of Use

Can We Go? is a personal and couple’s list. You are responsible for what you save and for who you invite.

- Invite only people you want in that library.
- Do not use the app to store anyone else’s private data without their say-so.
- We may refuse or rate-limit parse and invite traffic that looks like abuse.
- The service is provided as-is. A failed parse is not a lost evening — you can still save the link or photo as-is.

Deleting your account is in Settings. What happens to a shared library is spelled out there before you confirm.
